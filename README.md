# Daily Quote

A black page with one white quote, a new one every time you open it (or tap / press →).

- 5,000 unique quotes on procrastination, not giving up, bravery, greatness, doing the right thing, delayed gratification, purpose, consistency and hard work.
- English (4,423), Hindi (358) and Urdu (219), with Hindi and Urdu written in Devanagari.
- Hand-picked "best" quotes come up 3x as often. The order is shuffled once and remembered, so you don't see repeats until you've gone through the set.
- Filters for language and theme. Works offline and can be added to your home screen.

Data: `quotes.json` is an array of `[text, author, lang, theme, best]`.
English quotes were filtered from the public Quotes-500K (Goodreads) dataset and well-known quote lists, then curated by hand.
